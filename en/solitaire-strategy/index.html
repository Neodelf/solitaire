Solitaire Strategy: How to Win More Games

Practical strategies to improve your win rate when playing free online Solitaire.

How to Beat Solitaire

Beating Klondike consistently means stacking small advantages: reveal hidden cards before burning the stock, keep a free column for Kings when possible, and avoid premature foundation moves that trap needed cards. Apply the tactics below on every deal, then play free Solitaire online to build muscle memory.

Best Solitaire Strategy Tips to Win More Games

Reveal Hidden Cards Early

Turning over face-down cards in the tableau early unlocks more move options. Move sequences to reveal the cards underneath — this is critical for game progress.

Prioritize Moves That Open New Tableau Columns

Opening an empty tableau spot makes the game easier; you can move Kings there and reorganize piles. Prefer moves that open new columns over simply moving to foundations.

Delay Moving to Foundations If It Restricts Tableau Options

Moving a card to a foundation is irreversible. If that move would reduce your options on the tableau, try other moves first. Klondike rules always allow moving to foundations; the strategy is knowing when to do it.

Manage the Stock Cycle Mindfully

Keep track of which cards you've drawn from the stock in your mind. Knowing where cards are helps you plan the tableau better. Avoid wasting the stock cycle unnecessarily.

Solitaire Strategy FAQ

What is the best Solitaire strategy for beginners?

Start by revealing hidden tableau cards as early as possible. This unlocks more legal moves and improves planning options.

Should I prioritize foundations or tableau moves?

Prioritize tableau moves when they open hidden cards or empty columns. Move to foundations when it does not reduce flexibility.

How do I use the stock pile strategically?

Track stock order and avoid cycling without evaluating tableau alternatives first. Better stock timing often creates decisive moves.
