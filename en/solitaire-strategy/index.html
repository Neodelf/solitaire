Solitaire Strategy

Practical strategies to improve your win rate when playing free online Solitaire.

Reveal Hidden Cards Early

Turning over face-down cards in the tableau early unlocks more move options. Move sequences to reveal the cards underneath — this is critical for game progress.

Prioritize Moves That Open New Tableau Columns

Opening an empty tableau spot makes the game easier; you can move Kings there and reorganize piles. Prefer moves that open new columns over simply moving to foundations.

Delay Moving to Foundations If It Restricts Tableau Options

Moving a card to a foundation is irreversible. If that move would reduce your options on the tableau, try other moves first. Klondike rules always allow moving to foundations; the strategy is knowing when to do it.

Manage the Stock Cycle Mindfully

Keep track of which cards you've drawn from the stock in your mind. Knowing where cards are helps you plan the tableau better. Avoid wasting the stock cycle unnecessarily.
