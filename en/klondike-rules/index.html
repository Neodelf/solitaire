Klondike Solitaire Rules

This guide explains the official Klondike Solitaire rules used in World Solitaire, including setup, legal moves, and stock cycle basics.

Rules of Klondike Solitaire (quick reference)

Tableau: build down in alternating colors. Empty columns: Kings only (or a sequence starting with a King). Foundations: build up by suit from Ace to King. Stock feeds the waste pile; the top waste card is playable. For a beginner-friendly walkthrough, see how to play Klondike Solitaire, then play free online.

How to Play Klondike: Objective and Setup

  • Deal seven tableau piles from one to seven cards, with only top cards face-up.
  • Place all remaining cards in the stock pile.
  • Build four foundations by suit from Ace to King.

Klondike Solitaire Rules for Legal Moves

  • Build tableau piles down in alternating colors.
  • Move sequences between tableau piles to reveal hidden cards.
  • Only Kings or King-starting sequences can fill empty tableau columns.
  • Draw from stock to waste when no tableau move is available.
  • Move cards to foundations when the move does not reduce tableau options.

Klondike Rules FAQ

What are the rules of Klondike Solitaire?

Build tableau piles down in alternating colors, move sequences to reveal hidden cards, place only Kings (or King-led sequences) in empty columns, and complete foundations by suit from Ace to King. Details on stock and waste are covered in the sections above.

Can you move any card to an empty tableau column?

No. In standard Klondike, only a King or a sequence starting with a King can be placed in an empty column.

What is the goal in Klondike Solitaire?

Move all cards to the four foundations by suit from Ace to King.

When should you move cards to foundations?

Usually after checking tableau impact. If foundation moves block reveal opportunities, delay them.
