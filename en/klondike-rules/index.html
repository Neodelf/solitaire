Klondike Solitaire Rules

This guide explains the official rules of Klondike Solitaire used in World Solitaire.

Objective

Build all four foundations by suit from Ace to King.

Moves

  • Move cards between tableau piles in descending order and alternating colors.
  • Only Kings or sequences starting with a King may be moved to empty tableau piles.
  • Flip cards from stock to waste when necessary.
  • Move cards to foundations as soon as it doesn’t reduce tableau options.
