How to Win at Solitaire: Klondike Checklist

This checklist is designed for players who already know the basic rules and want to win more often. Keep it open in a second tab while you play. For fundamentals, start with How to play Klondike Solitaire.

Priority checklist (top to bottom)

  1. Reveal face-down cards in the tableau as early as possible.
  2. Preserve at least one empty tableau column for King mobility.
  3. Avoid moving low cards to foundations too early if tableau flexibility drops.
  4. Before drawing from stock, scan for sequence moves in tableau.
  5. When two legal moves exist, choose the move that opens more future options.
  6. Track color balance to avoid dead-end runs in tableau columns.

Common anti-patterns

  • Foundation rush: moving everything up too fast and blocking tableau rebuilding.
  • Stock spam: cycling stock without checking a single deep tableau move.
  • Column lock: filling your only empty column with a low-value King sequence.
  • Undo drift: using undo without a clear tactical objective.

60-second move audit

When stuck, ask these in order:

  1. Can I reveal a hidden card in one or two moves?
  2. Can I create or preserve an empty column?
  3. Does this foundation move reduce my tableau options?
  4. If I draw stock now, what exact card am I hoping to unlock?
