How to Play Solitaire Online (Klondike)

Klondike Solitaire is the classic solitaire most players know. This quick guide covers setup, official rules, and practical tips to win more often.

Setup

  • Deal seven tableau piles: from one to seven cards, only the top card face-up.
  • The remaining cards form the stock.
  • Foundations start with Aces and build up by suit to Kings.

Rules

  • Build tableau piles down in alternating colors (e.g., red 7 on black 8).
  • Move sequences between tableau piles to reveal hidden cards.
  • Empty tableau spots can be filled with a King or a sequence starting with a King.
  • Move cards to foundations in ascending order by suit from Ace to King.

Winning Tips

  • Prioritize revealing face-down cards in the tableau.
  • Avoid blocking low-value cards under high ones when possible.
  • Use undo and hints sparingly to improve efficiency.

How to Win at Solitaire (Klondike)

Winning is not guaranteed on every deal, but strong habits raise your success rate: open hidden cards before cycling the stock repeatedly, protect empty columns for Kings, and delay foundation moves that lock the tableau. How to Play Klondike Solitaire: FAQ

What is the fastest way to learn how to play Klondike Solitaire?

Memorize setup, legal moves, and foundation order. Then focus on revealing hidden cards quickly.

Can I win every Solitaire game?

No, but practicing on winnable deals helps improve consistency and planning.

Should I move cards to foundations immediately?

Only when it does not block better tableau moves. Tableau flexibility often matters more.

How do you win at Solitaire more often?

Focus on revealing face-down cards, avoid blocking low cards, and use foundations when they do not remove critical tableau options. The strategy guide expands on each habit.
