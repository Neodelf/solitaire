                            How to play Klondike Solitaire

                            1. Deal the cards: Seven tableau piles are dealt with increasing cards; remaining cards go to stock.
                            2. Build tableau: Build down in alternating colors. Move sequences between piles to reveal new cards.
                            3. Move to foundations: Start from Aces and build up by suit to Kings in four foundation piles.
                            4. Use stock wisely: Draw from stock when stuck and look for opportunities to free hidden cards.
